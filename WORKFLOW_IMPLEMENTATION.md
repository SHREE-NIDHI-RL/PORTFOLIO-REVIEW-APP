## 🔄 Complete Workflow

### Step 1: Owner Sends Request
1. Owner goes to "Find Reviewer"
2. Selects a reviewer and portfolio
3. Request is sent with owner profile and portfolio content

### Step 2: Reviewer Receives & Reviews
1. Reviewer sees request in "Review Requests" with full portfolio content
2. Reviewer can accept or reject the request
3. After accepting, reviewer submits detailed review with score (1-10) and feedback

### Step 3: Owner Receives Review
1. Completed review appears in owner dashboard
2. Owner can see reviewer profile, score, and detailed feedback
3. Owner can post the review to community using "Post Review" button

### Step 4: Community Sharing
1. Posted reviews appear in community feed
2. All users (owners and reviewers) can see posts on their dashboards
3. Posts include portfolio info, reviewer details, and review content
